## Hypothesis testing

Using Official FIFA World Cup matches after 2002-01-01, assuming each match is fully independent and team form is ignored.

-   Are more goals scored in women's international soccer matches than men's?

-   We assume a 10% significance level and define the following hypotheses:

**𝐻₀**: 𝜇_women = 𝜇_men

**𝐻ₐ**: 𝜇_women > 𝜇_men

Where:

𝐻₀ is the null hypothesis: the mean number of goals scored in women’s matches is equal to that in men’s matches.

𝐻ₐ is the alternative hypothesis: the mean number of goals scored in women’s matches is greater than that in men’s matches.
